Add either URL to your feed reader (Feedly, NetNewsWire, Miniflux, …); both carry the same posts. Atom is the safer default — JSON Feed support is less widespread.

Full history

The subscription feed carries the current year (at least the 20 most recent posts). Everything older lives in per-year archive documents, published under RFC 5005: follow the prev-archive link from atom.xml to walk back through archive-YYYY.xml. Closed years never change.

Few readers walk those links automatically, so you can also fetch an archive directly, as archive-YYYY.xml (Atom) or archive-YYYY.json (JSON Feed).
